Post-Session

Aftercare Guide

01
🩹
Keep It Covered

Leave the wrap on for 2–4 hours. If we used a second-skin bandage, keep it on for 3–5 days and let it breathe naturally.

02
🧼
Clean Gently

Wash twice daily with fragrance-free soap and lukewarm water. Pat dry — never rub. No submerging in water for 2 weeks.

03
💧
Moisturise

Apply a thin layer of unscented lotion (Lubriderm or Aveeno) 2–3× daily. Avoid petroleum jelly or heavily scented products.

04
☀️
Sun & Water

No direct sun for 4 weeks. After healing, always use SPF 50+ on tattoos. No pools, hot tubs, or ocean for 2–3 weeks.

Common Questions

FAQ

Does it hurt?

All tattoos involve some discomfort. Pain level varies greatly by placement — ribs, spine, and feet tend to be more intense; arms and thighs less so. Our artists work at a pace that keeps you comfortable.

How long does healing take?

Surface healing takes 2–3 weeks. Full dermal healing takes 3–4 months. During this time the tattoo may peel and look slightly dull — that's normal. Follow our aftercare guide carefully.

Can I bring reference images?

Absolutely — and we encourage it. Bring photos of styles you love, placements you've seen, and even images with zero tattoo context (landscapes, art, anything inspiring). The more context, the better the outcome.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule 48+ hours before your appointment and your deposit can be transferred to a new date. Cancellations under 48 hours forfeit the deposit. No-shows are not rebooked.

Do you do walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome on Saturdays only, subject to artist availability. Custom designs always require a prior consultation. We recommend calling ahead even for walk-in days.

What health conditions affect tattooing?

Certain conditions (blood thinners, diabetes, skin disorders, pregnancy) may affect your ability to be tattooed or your healing. Always disclose medical conditions at consultation so we can advise properly.
